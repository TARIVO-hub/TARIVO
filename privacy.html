Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information TARIVO collects and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Social account connection. When the TARIVO account owner connects a TikTok or Instagram account to our posting tool, we receive basic profile information (such as the account name) and an access token that lets us publish content to that account.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message.
  • Website data. This website is a static information page and does not ask visitors for personal details.

2. How we use it

  • To publish TARIVO's own content to its own accounts.
  • To reply to messages sent to us.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not use TikTok or Instagram data to build profiles of other users, and we do not read other people's private data.

4. Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored by the tools we use to connect our accounts and are used only to publish our content. We share information only with those service providers or when the law requires it.

5. Your choices

The TARIVO account owner can disconnect the connection at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings, which ends our access. To ask about or request deletion of information we hold about you, email us.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it was last changed.

7. Contact
